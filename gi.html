GOAT Index

GI is COURTSIDE’s proprietary analytical scale for comparing players within a season and across eras.

Important: GI is not official NBA statistics and is not a replacement for traditional metrics. It is an authorial model designed to structure comparison, not to declare absolute truth.

Seasonal components

How it works

Each player receives component scores based on available counting stats, advanced estimates and role context. The weighted sum produces a seasonal GI score used for the Top 100 ranking.

Historical rankings (see GOAT page) add peak windows, longevity, championship contribution, playoff impact and relative dominance within an era.

Weights and inputs can be adjusted. The goal is transparency of reasoning, not a black-box “super algorithm.”
