GOAT Index

GI is COURTSIDE’s proprietary scale for comparing players. It is not official NBA statistics and does not replace traditional metrics.

GI combines production, efficiency, creation, defense, consistency and winning impact. Historical rankings add peak, longevity, championships and era context. Always interpret with the accompanying explanation.
